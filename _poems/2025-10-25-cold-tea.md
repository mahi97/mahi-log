I am living at DGIST, a very calm campus, far from all the city noise and madness.
I like here we have a very good nature, clean air, calm mornings and peacful nights.
I still did not buy a TV, and it actually freed a lot of my attention and time at home, instead of listening to some background noise all day while working, now I am left with my own thoughts and memories, which is a bit uncomfortable and annoying from time to time but at the end of the day I am happier this way.

One thing I realized is that I really get into peotry and I want to read and write poems and this blog section is an attempt for me to collecting all these poems here.

I don't think about what to write and I just come up with whatever that I was feeling or thinking deeply that day about it and put it in some nice verse, I do not think they are qualified to be called peoms, however that make me feel better to call them peoms so I do it anyway.

I write all peomes originally in Persian, however I try to leave a translation for it, in case I think many AI translations can not capture the true meaning.



This is my first peom:


چایم سرد شد، دوباره چای را دم کردم 
گل‌رز، دارچین، هل‌دانه، و یک حبه نبات
به یادِ شیرینی لبخندت.

اما جای خالی‌ات را
هیچ‌کدام پر نمی‌کنند.
لحظه‌ها کند می‌گذرند —
هر روز، هفته‌ای‌ست،
و هر ماه، سالی‌ست بی‌تو.
گاهی شب‌ها خوابی می‌بینم
که تو در آنی —
کمی از دلتنگی‌ام می‌کاهد.
گاهی می‌خندی،
گاهی می‌رقصی،
گاهی می‌بخشیم.
اما سحرگاه،
خواب تو را از من می‌گیرد،
و من دقیقه‌ها، گاهی ثانیه‌ها را می‌شمارم
تا دوباره خوابت را ببینم.
اما هنوز هم بدقولی
گاهی نمی‌آیی،
و من تمام شب در انتظارِ آمدنت میشینم.
انتظاری که خودش هم زیباست،
چون من شدیدا دلتنگ به انتظارت نشستن ام.
چهار ماه از آخرین باری که دیدمت میگذرد
 از آخرین قطرات اشکت
وقتی در اتوبوس نشستی
و برای خداحافظی دست تکان دادی.
نمی‌دانستم آخرین بار است.
کاش محکم‌تر در آغوش گرفته بودمت،
کاش دستت را دیرتر رها می‌کردم،
کاش همه‌ی چهره‌ات را غرق بوسه می‌کردم
و در نگاهت غرق می‌شدم.
کاش امروز می‌دانستم که حالت خوب است،
کاش میدانستم که هنوز هم می‌خندی، می‌رقصی، خوشی،
و همه‌ی این‌ها فقط رؤیای من نیستند.
دیدی دوباره تمام حواسم را گرفتی؟
چایم دوباره سرد شد...


English Translations:

My tea went cold, so I brewed it once more:
rose petals, cinnamon, cardamom,
and a cube of sugar, to remind myself of your sweet memory.

Yet none of these fill the space you’ve left.

Moments crawl;
each day stretches into a week,
each month — a year of waiting.

Sometimes at night, I dream you’re there —
it soothes my longing, just a little.
Sometimes you laugh,
sometimes you dance,
sometimes you forgive me.
But the dawn takes you away again.
And I count minutes — sometimes seconds —
until sleep returns,
until I see you once more.

Yet you still don't keep promises, sometimes you are late,
sometimes you never come,
and I spend the whole night awake,
waiting for you —
a beautiful pain, perhaps,
because I truly miss the waiting itself for you.
Four months passed since the last time I saw your face —
since your tears fell
and you waved goodbye from the bus window.

If only I had known it was the last time —
I’d have held you tighter,
kept your hands longer in mine,
kissed your face all over,
and drowned in your eyes once more.

I wish I knew you were well —
that you are happy,
that you still laugh from your heart,
that you still dance —
and that these are not just my dreams.

See what’s happened again?
My tea has gone cold again,
its warmth forgotten.

I must heat it once more.
