Poems

I live at DGIST, a very calm campus, far from the city's noise and madness. I like it here; we have a very good nature, clean air, calm mornings, and peaceful nights. I still did not buy a TV, and it actually freed a lot of my attention and time at home, instead of listening to some background noise all day while working, now I am left with my own thoughts and memories, which is a bit uncomfortable and annoying from time to time, but at the end of the day I am happier this way. One thing I realized is that I really get into poetry, and I want to read and write poems. This blog section is an attempt to collect all these poems here. I don't think about what to write, and I just come up with whatever I was feeling or thinking deeply about that day, and put it in some nice verses. They are not qualified to be called poems; however, that makes me feel better to call them poems, so I do it anyway. I write all my poems originally in Persian; however, I try to include a translation, in case I think many AI translations cannot capture the true meaning.
